## Pattern Summary: Common Structures in Premeditated Property Crime

| Element | Pattern |
|---------|---------|
| **Trust Betrayal** | Fellow villagers, fellow travelers, and fellow inn-guests exploit information advantages to steal or defraud |
| **Wealth Exposure** | Victims actively or passively expose their property scale, becoming targeting signals |
| **Legal Consumption** | Drag disputes into litigation, using time costs and financial drain to wear down the opponent |
| **Emotional Manipulation** | Exploit arrogance, greed, and fear to push victims into irrational decisions |
| **Collective Collusion** | Structural advantage of many-against-one: blood oaths across the ship, coordinated perjury, shared loot |
| **Information Blockade** | Victims refuse market signals (Yu Dingzhi) or misread risk signals (Chao Shicai) |
| **Modern Equivalent** | Partners absconding with funds, commercial litigation to ruin opponents, stock-market套牢, P2P bank runs |

## Appendix: Ming Dynasty Slang Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **棍 / 光棍** | Swindler, rogue (Ming street slang) |
| **伙** | Accomplice, partner in crime |
| **牙** | Broker, middleman |
| **衙役** | Government runner, bailiff |
| **棍徒** | Gang member, thug |
| **作契对银** | Formal contract and silver exchange (standard Ming transaction) |
| **打抽丰 / 秋风** | Seasonal grafting — exploiting official connections for money/gifts |
| **龟奴** | Brothel pimp, procurer |
