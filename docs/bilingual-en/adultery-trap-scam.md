## Pattern Summary: Common Structures in Adultery-Trap Scam

| Element | Pattern |
|---------|---------|
| **Target Selection** | Select men with strong libido and poor self-control, especially those traveling or trading away from home; exploit "momentary impulse" to reduce rational judgment |
| **Bait Deployment** | Use women (maids, tenant wives, broker daughters, nuns) as information channels and seduction media; women may be victims, accomplices, or tools |
| **Window Creation** | Carefully design spatial-temporal windows: "nobody home," "husband away," "late night alone"—making the target believe the opportunity is natural |
| **On-site Capture** | Catching in bed is the critical node for legal and financial勒索; "caught on the spot" had special legal force in Ming law |
| **Identity Reversal** | Reframe主动勾引 as "being victimized" (for the woman) or "resisting exposure" (for the man); exploit gender norms and class differences to manipulate justice |
| **Revenue Maximization** | Extract multiple revenues from a single sexual transaction: sexual gratification (for the accomplice woman), monetary勒索 (Liao San model), bloodline theft (Lu Dili model), geomancy grafting (geomancer model) |
| **Modern Equivalent** | Honey traps, sex-based extortion,钓鱼执法, workplace sexual harassment counterclaims, sextortion |
