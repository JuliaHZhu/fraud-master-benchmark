## Pattern Summary: Common Structures in Strip-and-Rob Scam

| Element | Pattern |
|---------|---------|
| **Core Mechanism** | Borrow others' identities, items, venues, and social relations as "cover," making fraud appear as normal activity to bystanders |
| **Information Advantage** | The scammer holds the "true intent"; victims and bystanders each hold fragments that cannot form a complete picture |
| **Key Move** | Create a "publicly credible narrative" (buying horses, depositing silver, repairing tables, saying farewell, mutual agreement) so third parties become unwitting witnesses |
| **Victim Blind Spot** | Victims lower their guard due to small gains (travel money, exchange-rate差价), social inertia (bowing, responding, hosting guests), or fixed routines (lunchtime) |
| **Modern Equivalent** | Asset stripping,名义 borrowing fraud, shell-company transactions, identity-theft loans, fake collaboration to seize assets |
