## Pattern Summary: Common Structures in Fake-Cleric Scam

| Element | Pattern |
|---------|---------|
| **Persona Packaging** | Use appearance (resembling a temple guardian), talent (preaching, fasting), and moral narratives (Mulian saving his mother, past-life ties) to build a superhuman image |
| **Authority Borrowing** | Borrow Buddhist scriptures, temple credibility, and karma frameworks to give the scam legitimacy beyond secular logic |
| **Information Gathering** | Eavesdrop, observe (cattle licking salt), pre-investigate (learn farming schedules), then package public info as "prophecy" |
| **Verification Dilemma** | Fasting and miracles are hard to verify under normal conditions; verification designs (locked doors, secret peeping) are defeated by props (infant pills, hidden food) |
| **Moral Coercion** | Lock victims in with religious language: "salvation," "saving people," "merit," "disaster removal"; refusing donation = refusing fortune / inviting disaster |
| **Crime Escalation** | From fraud (stealing cattle, gold) to sexual assault (the Wuer case) to murder (infant pills, the Wuer case); religious identity provides cover |
| **Modern Equivalent** | Fake living Buddhas, qigong masters, spiritual mentors, charitable monks; temple donation scams, consecration profiteering, religious PUA |
