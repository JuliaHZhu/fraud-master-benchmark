## Pattern Summary: Common Structures in Poetry-Scholar Impersonation

| Element | Pattern |
|---------|---------|
| **Cultural Capital Arbitrage** | Use poetic talent, calligraphy, and celebrity identity as cultural capital to gain trust or resources |
| **Identity Dislocation** | Exploit the gap between real identity (talented scholar, celebrity) and disguised identity (Taoist, wealthy merchant) |
| **Power Intermediation** | Use officials' administrative power to convert personal fraud into government fiscal expenditure |
| **Time Gap** | Exploit delays in information transmission to complete consumption and escape before the truth is exposed |
| **Authority Indulgence** | Victims (officials) actively放弃追责 due to "cherishing talent," making the fraud zero-cost |
| **Modern Equivalent** | Celebrity endorsement fraud, academic fraud套经费, artist-identity scams, NFT celebrity endorsement scams |
