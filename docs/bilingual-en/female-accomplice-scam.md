## Pattern Summary: Common Structures in Female-Accomplice Scam

| Element | Pattern |
|---------|---------|
| **Gender Leverage** | Use female identity to break social barriers (sisters-in-law, daughters-in-law, nuns, lone women), entering trust spaces men cannot reach |
| **Body Capitalization** | Use beauty/sex as a trading medium—not the endpoint, but the means to acquire goods (oil, meat, land deeds, horses, long-term support) |
| **Family Collusion** | Woman scams are rarely individual acts; they are coordinated family/team operations (tenant families, three-woman分工, nun-prostitute alliances) |
| **Emotional Manipulation** | Exploit shared misery, jealousy, and spring-longing to drag victims from rational judgment into emotional decisions |
| **Information Isolation** | Core manipulators (Zuo Shi, tenant mother, Miaozhen) always hide behind front-stage executors (Shi Shi, daughter-in-law, Xiang Shi) |
| **Second Harvest** | Not only acquire material benefits but also extract extra value through intimidation and emotional binding (thank-you gifts, land deeds, bracelets) |
| **Modern Equivalent** | Marriage bait scams, wine/meal bait scams, honey traps, emotional fraud, female roles in pig-butchering scams |
