## Pattern Summary: Common Structures in Kidnapping Scam

| Element | Pattern |
|---------|---------|
| **Target Selection** | Specifically wait for lost children around ten years old, or deceive poor families into selling their children |
| **Value Grading** | Pretty and clever ones are sold to brothels; dull ones are blinded or maimed and forced to beg |
| **Violence Manufacturing** | Use extreme torture (blinding, amputation, poisoning) to create disability or death, eliminating resistance |
| **Industry Chain** | "Abduct-disable-beg" or "abduct-sell-cook" form complete industry chains; dispersed by day, gathered by night |
| **Judicial Capture** | Bribe officials and relatives with silver so victims lose their last institutional remedy |
| **Information Black Hole** | Families who sold their children "disappear without a trace"; information is completely blocked, parents remain ignorant for long periods |
| **Modern Equivalent** | Cross-border human trafficking, forced-begging gangs, organ-trafficking black markets |
