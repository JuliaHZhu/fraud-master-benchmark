## Pattern Summary: Common Structures in Broker Scam

| Element | Pattern |
|---------|---------|
| **Mediator Role** | Brokers (牙行) act as intermediaries between buyers and sellers, mastering information asymmetry |
| **Debt Misappropriation** | Use later clients' goods to pay earlier clients' debts, forming a Ponzi-style debt chain |
| **Identity Forgery** | Use "ghost names" and fake names to fabricate transaction subjects, making pursuit impossible |
| **Delay Tactics** | Use "borrowed批" and "extensions" to wear down creditors' patience,诱导 them to accept non-monetary solutions |
| **Human Collateral** | Use daughters or people as debt collateral, converting commercial debt into human trafficking |
| **Judicial Capture** | Bribe officials and relatives with silver to deadlock or bias lawsuits in favor of the debtor |
| **Victim Psychology** | Merchants' eagerness to liquidate is exploited, sliding them from "debt collection" to "accepting alternative solutions" |
| **Modern Equivalent** | Freight forwarders absconding with funds, agents misappropriating client capital, P2P Ponzi schemes, human-trafficking debt bondage |
