## Pattern Summary: Common Structures in Silver-Swap Scam

| Element | Pattern |
|---------|---------|
| **Trust Building** | Establish初步 trust through fellow-traveler or fellow-villager identity, or demonstrate sincerity with real silver first |
| **Contact Legitimization** | Use "money exchange" or "borrowing to inspect" as transactional pretexts to gain legitimate contact with real silver/gold |
| **Switch Mechanism** | During turning away, dim light, or distracted attention, substitute fake silver/copper ingots for the real goods |
| **Spatial Isolation** | Quickly disembark and leave after success, using physical distance to block pursuit |
| **History Exploitation** | If the first attempt fails, track to the ship for a second attempt, forming a "follow-up" pattern |
| **Victim Psychology** | Overconfidence ("What swindler could fool me?") and information-advantage illusion (thinking they are the ones inspecting the silver) |
| **Modern Equivalent** | Counterfeit currency scams, precious-metal switching, ATM fake bills, fake cryptocurrency wallets |
