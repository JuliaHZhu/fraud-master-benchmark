## Pattern Summary: Common Structures in Fake-Friendship Scam

| Element | Pattern |
|---------|---------|
| **Target Selection** | Select wealthy or promising acquaintances with personality weaknesses: drunkard, foolish, charitable, or frivolous |
| **Long-term Infiltration** | Build trust through fake intimacy over months or years |
| **Precision Weakness Exploitation** | Wine, sex, gambling, lawsuits—continuously induce the target along their most vulnerable dimension |
| **Reverse Incentive** | Appear to dissuade while actually using provocation and competitive spirit to push the victim into the trap |
| **Multi-role Play** | One person simultaneously plays friend, mediator, creditor, and buyer, harvesting at every stage |
| **Liability Separation** | Every key step is the victim's "voluntary" choice; the fraudster merely offers "advice" or "company" |
| **Modern Equivalent** | Acquaintance pig-butchering scams, PUA emotional manipulation, workplace sabotage, investment-group shills |
