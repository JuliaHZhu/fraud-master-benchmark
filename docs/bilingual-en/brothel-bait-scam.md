## Pattern Summary: Common Structures in Brothel-Bait Scam

| Element | Pattern |
|---------|---------|
| **Bait Design** | Use beauty as bait, exploiting the victim's sexual desire or emotional needs |
| **Identity Disguise** | Prostitutes play "accidental encounter" or "forced into prostitution" weak roles to lower the victim's guard |
| **Psychological Manipulation** | Four-step induction: guilt → moved → indulgence → sunk cost, making the victim actively consume |
| **Insider Collusion** | Servants and maids are bribed to dismantle the victim's rational defenses from within |
| **Asset Harvesting** | Gradually drain the victim's capital through prostitution fees, gifts, and banquets |
| **Modern Equivalent** | Sex-induction investment scams, honey traps, wine/meal bait scams |
