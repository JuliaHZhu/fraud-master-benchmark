## Pattern Summary: Common Structures in Gambling-Bait Scam

| Element | Pattern |
|---------|---------|
| **Bait Design** | Activate the victim's vanity and competitive spirit with identity labels like "hero," "real man," "smart person" |
| **Provocation Mechanism** | Use social anxiety like "taking orders from lowlifes" or "being laughed at" to force the victim to prove autonomy by gambling again |
| **Scene Construction** | Use prostitutes and fake young masters as intermediaries to create scripted "chance encounter-rivalry-gambling" narratives |
| **Gambling Control** | Control dice, betting pace, and interruption timing to create the illusion of a "luck reversal" |
| **Social Engineering Prelude** | Pre-bribe onlookers and villagers to not dissuade but "approve of his gambling" and "provoke him with words" |
| **Retreat Script** | Forcefully terminate before the victim is completely ruined using excuses like "father's orders" or "late night"—avoiding conflict while leaving room for future scams |
| **Modern Equivalent** | Pig-butchering scams, gambling-induction fraud, live-stream打赏 induction, game-recharge induction |
