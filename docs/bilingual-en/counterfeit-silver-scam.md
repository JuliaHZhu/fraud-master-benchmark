## Pattern Summary: Common Structures in Counterfeit-Silver Scam

| Element | Pattern |
|---------|---------|
| **Target Selection** | Select trading partners lacking professional verification ability or restricted verification means (village farmers, long-distance merchants, market trades without broker担保) |
| **Trust Building** | Actively offer samples for inspection (real first, fake later), forge kinship ties, leverage熟人背书 |
| **Technical Barrier** | Counterfeiting expertise far exceeds individual鉴别能力; surface texture, weight, and sound can all be faked |
| **Verification Dilemma** | Destructive testing (cutting, chiseling) is unacceptable in trade; non-destructive tests (color, silk pattern) are fooled by surface treatment |
| **Time Lag Design** | The victim leaves the scene (to take up a post or trade elsewhere) before discovering the fraud; the scammer has already escaped |
| **Moral Narrative Substitution** | Replace structural analysis with "divine retribution" narratives ("heaven watches," "descendants will not prosper") to avoid institutional solutions |
| **Modern Equivalent** | Counterfeit currency, fake gold,山寨 cryptocurrency, phishing-site SSL certificate forgery |
